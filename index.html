02 / A little about me

ABOUT ME

I started my journey at Source Kode Solutions as a Data Entry Intern in June 2023 and grew into an E-Commerce Product Listing Executive.

My experience spans product listings for SKINEVE, VapeGala, GreetVape, Ejuice Couture, Hillcrest Clouds, Luxury Desires, and Alectrofag. Alongside this, I have managed complete operations for SKINEVE and worked on website development for SKINEVE and my own brand, BASIM.

My work brings together accurate product data, clear content, visual presentation, and practical store operations. I'm also exploring how AI and automation can improve everyday e-commerce processes.
